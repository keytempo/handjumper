# Hand Jumper

> A high-quality panel archive for Hand Jumper fans — because the series is GOATED and deserves better than a screenshot of a screenshot.

An unofficial, non-commercial fan project that archives **Hand Jumper** episodes as individual panels, complete with episode metadata — built to make high-quality references easy for the community.

## Why this exists

If you've ever tried to reference a specific panel for a Reddit post, an analysis thread, or a meme, you know the problem: everything in circulation is a screenshot of a screenshot, and quality drops with every share. This archive gives the community a proper source — every panel preserved individually, ready to be read, referenced, shared, cropped, and combined.

That's useful whether you're:

- referencing a moment in a Reddit post
- analyzing a scene or character
- making a meme
- stitching a few panels together for context
- tracking down a specific reaction or expression
- setting a panel as your wallpaper or PFP
- revisiting a moment without scrubbing through a full episode
- sharing a clean reference with someone else
- or just rereading, because **Hand Jumper is GOATED**

## Quality

Every panel is archived at peak quality — losslessly compressed to the smallest file size that quality allows, so nothing is lost to compression artifacts.

## Always up to date

This isn't a one-time snapshot. The archive updates automatically as new episodes release, so the collection stays current without a manual rebuild.

Episode metadata — numbering, titles, and panel counts — is maintained alongside the panels.

## The reader

The archive has a purpose-built reader, so you can browse for the panel you want and grab it without having to dig through the raw archive yourself:

**https://handjumper.pages.dev/?ep=latest**

Find it, download it, and use it however you need — crop it, stitch panels together for context, show the exact panel instead of describing it, or turn one into a wallpaper or PFP.
